Two separate forms is the right way to do this, and it does not change the cost or the time. One form is for outside interests. The other is for skills. Someone who needs to record another company opens only that form. They do not go through the skills questions. Both still sit in the Microsoft 365 you already pay for. There is no new subscription and no monthly fee, and setup is still two to three working days of our time, quoted before we start.

It is not Microsoft Forms. A Microsoft Form keeps every submission as a new response, so a correction becomes a second row. What we put in Teams is a simple screen that opens the entry already there and saves back to it. I have attached four pictures of a rough example. The names and the questions on it are made up, so you can see the shape. The first picture is the choice of which form to open. The second is an interest that is already on the record, with Update and Add another company. The third is a skills question in the pattern you described: No, Would like to, or Yes, and a details box when the answer is Yes, plus a dropdown-only question and a free-text question. The fourth is what a named person sees for one member of staff, skills together and interests separate, with a download.

On Excel: there is no live link. The download is a copy. Editing that file does not change the records, and the records do not change the file until someone downloads a fresh copy. We will not use the Microsoft export that stays connected.

The copy has two sheets, and the headings stay the same every time, so you can paste them onto two data tabs and refresh your pivots without cleaning the columns.

- Skills: Name, Question, Answer, Details, Last updated. One row per answer. A new question adds rows. It does not change the shape of the sheet.
- Interests: Name, Company, Role, Status, Last updated, Notes. One row per company.

The first time, you build the pivot tables against those tabs. After that, replace the rows and refresh.

Yes, please send the skills questions when you have them. The mix you described is what that form will do: a choice, free text, or a choice followed by details. We do not need the list in order for you to sign the note off internally. We do need the wording before we build the skills form, so we know which answers should open a details box.

The updated note for circulation is the same proposal, with these points written in. Happy to talk it through.
