## 1. The requirements

Principia wants this inside the Microsoft 365 the firm already uses:

1. A member of staff can record a conflict of interest. The example given is work for another company.
2. A member of staff can keep their skills up to date.
3. These are two different jobs. Someone recording a company does not have to go through the skills questions.
4. They change the entry they already made. A correction does not create a second copy.
5. All of one person's skills sit together, so a reader sees that person, not a list sorted by the time things were typed in.
6. Each person sees only their own skills and their own interests.
7. A small number of named people can see everyone's records.
8. Skills questions will be a mix. Some are a dropdown. Some are free text. Some are both, for example "No / Would like to / Yes", and if the answer is Yes, a box for brief details.
9. The named people can download a spreadsheet and build pivot tables in their own Excel file. That file is a copy. It is not linked live to the records.
10. There is no new Microsoft subscription and no monthly fee per person.

About 50 people will use it.

## 2. The design

There are two forms in Microsoft Teams. One for outside interests. One for skills. Staff open the one they need. The records sit in Principia's own Microsoft 365. Pro Drive sets this up. Principia owns the records.

This stays on the Microsoft 365 you already pay for. Splitting the forms does not add a Microsoft licence or a monthly fee, and it stays inside the same two to three working days.

These are not Microsoft Forms. A Microsoft Form stores a new response every time someone presses submit, so a correction becomes a second row. The screens in Teams open the entry that is already there and save back to it.

**Who sees what.**

| Who | What they see |
|-----|----------------|
| Each employee | Only their own skills and their own interests |
| Two or three people you name | Everyone's records, one person at a time, and a spreadsheet download |
| Anyone else | Nothing of a colleague's record |

The named people will see outside interests. Keep that group small.

If the person who allocates work needs to see skills, they can be given skills only. They see outside interests only if you also name them in the small group. You can decide that after sign-off.

**How a record is kept.**

- Outside interests are one entry per company. A correction updates that entry. A new company is a new entry. When an interest ends, the person marks it ended. The entry stays. People cannot delete it.
- If someone has no current interest, they confirm that on one entry and update the date when they review it. If they already have a current interest, that confirmation is not on the page.
- Skills are one answer per question. Saving again updates that answer. A choice question can have a details box that shows only when the answer needs it, as in your example.

**The spreadsheet.**

The download is a copy. It does not stay connected to Teams. Editing the Excel file does not change the records, and the records do not change the Excel file until someone downloads a fresh copy.

There are two sheets, with the same headings every time:

- Skills: Name, Question, Answer, Details, Last updated. One row per answer, so a new question adds rows and does not reshape the sheet.
- Interests: Name, Company, Role, Status, Last updated, Notes. One row per company.

Paste each sheet onto its own data tab in your workbook, then refresh the pivot tables. The first time, you build the pivots. After that you do not clean or rename columns. We will not use the Microsoft export that keeps a live link.

**Optional extra, decided after the forms are in use.** People who already have Microsoft Copilot can also update their own record in a private Teams chat. We switch that on only after we check the licence. If it does not cover the chat, the chat stays off.

## 3. What people do

**A member of staff** opens Teams and picks Outside interests or Skills. They do not have to open both.

On Outside interests they see companies they have already recorded. They update one, or they add another. To close one they mark it ended. The entry remains. If they have a current interest, the page does not offer "nothing to declare".

On Skills they see each question and the answer already saved. They change an answer and save. Where a question is "No / Would like to / Yes", the details box is there when the answer is Yes. A free-text question is a box they can leave blank.

They never see a colleague's page.

**A named person** picks a person and sees that person's skills together, and that person's interests separately. They download the spreadsheet copy when they want to refresh the pivot workbook.
