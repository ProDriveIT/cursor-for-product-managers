# A private record of outside interests and skills

**To:** Principia Advisory  
**From:** Pro Drive  
**Date:** 23 October 2026

Each person keeps their own record of outside interests, such as work for another company, and their own skills. They update the entry they already have. They do not submit a fresh form every time. A small group you name can see the whole firm, one person at a time. Colleagues cannot open each other's records.

This uses the Microsoft 365 you already pay for. There is no new Microsoft subscription and no monthly fee per person.

## What your people do

They open one form in Teams.

**Skills.** They see the skills already on their record. They change the level or the notes and save. To add a skill, they pick from a dropdown. A skill they already have does not appear again, so they cannot create a second copy by mistake.

**Outside interests.** They see each interest they have already recorded. They correct it on that same entry. A new interest is added only when it is actually new. When an interest ends, they mark it ended. They cannot delete it. The record stays.

**Nothing to declare.** If they have no current outside interest, they confirm that on a single entry and update the date when they have reviewed it. If they already have a current interest, that confirmation is hidden. They review the interests on the screen instead. Saying "nothing to declare" does not wipe an interest that is already there.

## Who can see what

| Who | What they see |
|-----|----------------|
| Each employee | Only their own skills and their own interests |
| The people you name, kept to a small group | Everyone's records, grouped by person, with the option to download a spreadsheet |
| Everyone else | Nothing of a colleague's record |

The people you name are administrators of this record. Choose them deliberately. Two or three is enough. They will see outside interests, which are private.

If someone who allocates work needs to see skills, tell us their name. We can let them see skills only. They will not see outside interests unless you also put them in the small admin group.

## Your choice

There is one way to do this that matches what you asked for and what you already pay Microsoft for. Everyone uses the form.

One optional extra, after the form is in use: people who already have Microsoft Copilot can also update their own record by chatting in Teams, one to one with an assistant. Colleagues still cannot see each other's records. We switch this on only after we have checked that the Copilot licence on those accounts covers it. If it does not, the chat stays off and everyone continues with the form. There is no second product to buy either way.

We are not proposing a separate system, a monthly charge per person, or a dashboard. A dashboard can be discussed later if a named person needs one. It would be a separate licence, so it is outside this proposal.

Once an administrator at Principia has given us access, we spend two to three working days setting it up and testing that one person cannot see another's records.

Five people then try the form. That takes a few minutes of each person's time. If four of the five can save a skill and either an interest or a "nothing to declare" confirmation, we open it to the rest of the firm. If nobody uses it, we stop before everyone is invited.
