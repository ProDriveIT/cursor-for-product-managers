## Workflow deviation

Nobody was asked, and nobody answered. The pushes below are the questions the brief cannot answer. Where the answer is missing, it stays missing. ## What the brief is

The sponsor asked for two capabilities, then specified the toolbox:

- Staff enter conflicts of interest into a register, for example when they work for other companies.
- Staff update a skills matrix.
- The build stays inside the company's Microsoft environment, uses Microsoft tools, and prefers the M365 Business Premium estate plus Copilot Studio.
- Azure spend is acceptable when the result is stronger, cheaper, or both, compared with extra per-user licences.
- Staff update an existing entry.
- Rows are grouped so one employee's skills sit together.
- An employee sees only their own skills or conflicts.
- Some fields are free text and some are dropdowns.
- Specified admins see all data.
- Reports and dashboards stay possible later.

Pro Drive context, section 1: Principia is a managed client, and Pro Drive sells managed IT to firms of this kind. Section 4, locked 19 September 2026: product capacity goes to the EOS Operating Dashboard, then client monthly reports, then self-service travel. This brief does not reorder that list. If it proceeds, it proceeds as professional services inside Principia's tenant.

The OKR folder has no current quarter objectives. There is no key result to score this against.

## Failure patterns

Four patterns are present. Each one is enough to stop a build.

1. **Two jobs bundled.** A conflicts register and a skills matrix are different jobs. A conflict row is sensitive (other employment, and any family interest the firm later adds). A skills matrix is how someone staffs work. The brief applies one privacy rule to both. That rule can serve the register and can empty the matrix of its staffing use.
2. **Hypothetical users.** "Staff" and "specified admins" are categories. No employee, no admin, no title, and no consequence is named. A category is a filter, not a customer.
3. **Interest mistaken for demand.** A requirements brief is a request. It is not evidence that anyone will keep a row current, chase a missing return, or get hurt when the register is wrong. Nobody has scrambled, paid, or built their week around this.
4. **Solution specified before the job.** Business Premium, Copilot Studio, Microsoft-only, and Azure-if-cheaper arrive before a named person, a current workaround, or a single observed task. Copilot Studio is named as a priority and is not an entitlement of Business Premium. The tool choice is doing the work the job description should do.

## Reframe

The strongest version of the sponsor's idea, stated without the toolbox:

Principia needs each in-scope person to hold a current conflict position that a named admin can produce later, and a skills record grouped by person. Colleagues do not read one another's rows.

That reframe is still unproven. "Needs" here means "the sponsor wrote it," not "a person demonstrated it."

The conflicts half is the one to pressure first. The brief's update rule is right for a skill and for a conflict that already exists. It is incomplete for a register. A year of silence is not evidence the person reviewed it. A nil return ("I have no undeclared conflicts") is the missing row. That judgment is Pro Drive's, and it is not a sentence in the brief. The sponsor may reject it.

The skills half is not a matrix until someone who staffs work can read across people. If the sponsor keeps skills as private as conflicts, call it a personal record and leave staffing outside the app.

## Forcing questions

### 1. Demand reality

**Question.** What is the strongest evidence someone would be genuinely upset if this disappeared tomorrow?

**Position.** There is no such evidence. The register does not exist, so "disappeared" does not apply. The brief does not describe a missed audit, a lost pitch, a wrong team, a fine, or a person chasing an answer. Sponsor interest is not demand.

**Pushes the brief cannot answer.** Who paid for this, or will sign a statement of work this week? Who expanded their own workaround until it broke? Who, by name, emails James if nothing ships in the next month? All three are blank.

**Evidence that would change this.** One named Principia person, a date in the last 12 months, and a consequence they bore because a conflict or a skill could not be produced. A signed statement of work with a price also counts. A second email that says the idea is useful does not count.

### 2. Status quo

**Question.** What are people doing now, even badly, and what does that workaround cost?

**Position.** Unknown. The brief does not mention a spreadsheet, a mailbox, an HR system, a partner, or "we do nothing." If the current solution is nothing, the pain may be too weak to build. That possibility stays open.

**Pushes.** Which file, list, or inbox? How many minutes on the last update? What error happened, and who noticed? None of these are in the source.

**Evidence that would change this.** A screen share of the current artifact, plus one counted cost from the last quarter (hours, a rework, a client question that went unanswered). A verbal description with no artifact and no count would not change it. We need the artifact and a count.

### 3. Desperate specificity

**Question.** Name the human who needs this most. What gets them promoted or fired?

**Position.** The human is unnamed. The brief's "staff" and "specified admins" cannot be interviewed, permissioned, or held accountable. Pro Drive must not invent a compliance officer, a managing partner, or a regulator to make the brief feel solid.

**Pushes.** Title? Name? What happens to that person if an outside directorship is missing from the pack they send a client? Still absent. A regulator is not cited, so this diagnostic does not treat the register as a legal control.

**Evidence that would change this.** One employee name and one admin name, each with the consequence of a wrong or missing row. A policy or contract citation can sit beside the names. It does not replace them.

### 4. Narrowest wedge

**Question.** What is the smallest version someone pays for this week?

**Position.** The paid wedge is a conflicts register in Principia's tenant: edit an existing interest in place, file an explicit nil return, hide rows from other employees, and let one named admin read the set grouped by person. Skills wait until a person who staffs work asks for them in the session below. Bundling both into the first build feels efficient and hides the privacy clash.

This is client work, so "pays" means Principia pays Pro Drive for the services, and Principia pays Microsoft for the tenant costs. No price has been asked for or agreed. The wedge is not a new Pro Drive SKU.

**Pushes.** What if the user had to do nothing? They cannot. Someone has to state the interest or state that there is none. A Copilot chat still needs that sentence, and Copilot Studio is a separate purchase unless Principia already pays for it or for Microsoft 365 Copilot. Zero-effort capture is the wrong test. The right test is one screen an employee can finish without a workshop.

**Evidence that would change this.** The sponsor shows a live staffing decision, with a date, that needs the skills view before the next conflicts cycle. Then skills move first. A request to "see the art of the possible" does not.

### 5. Observation and surprise

**Question.** Have you watched someone do this without helping? What surprised you?

**Position.** No. There is no session, no recording, and no surprise. A written brief cannot show a workaround the sponsor forgot to mention. Demos we run later will teach us nothing about demand.

**Pushes.** Not applicable. There is no first observation to push on.

**Evidence that would change this.** Sit behind one employee and one admin. They use whatever they use today. Pro Drive does not drive the mouse. Write down what they click and where they get stuck. One surprise in that session outweighs the brief.

### 6. Future-fit

**Question.** In three years, does this become more essential or less?

**Position.** The job can stay essential if Principia still does work where an outside interest matters. The brief does not say what Principia advises, so that business claim is open. The tool path can become a liability on a shorter clock. Dataverse for Teams is deprecated. New environments cannot be created from 15 November 2026, which is 24 days after this brief. Environments that are not upgraded are suspended on 15 September 2027 and decommissioned on 30 October 2027. After an upgrade, users need Power Apps, Power Automate, or Copilot Studio licences. A Copilot-first front door becomes less appropriate, not more. Dropdowns and a grouped matrix are a form. Chat is a poor system of record.

**Pushes.** "AI gets better" is a rising-tide argument any vendor can make. It does not pick Copilot Studio. "Every firm will need a skills matrix" is the same class of claim. Reject both.

**Evidence that would change this.** A Principia policy, in writing, that these records must be captured through an internal agent, plus proof they already pay for Copilot Studio or Microsoft 365 Copilot. For the business job: a client contract or internal policy that names conflicts or skills as something they must produce. Still no invented regulator.

## Weakest link

The weakest link is a missing person and a missing workaround, tied together. Without them, every architecture discussion is a quote for a hypothetical user. Licensing math can still be prepared, because the prices are public. The build cannot.

Company-fit check, separate from demand: even a proven demand does not jump the locked work order. James staffs this from client delivery capacity, or he declines it. Evidence that would change the capacity call: James reorders the work order in writing.

## One assignment

Before any build, any environment, and any client quote, James Stock books one working session with the Principia sponsor and one employee the sponsor names.

The employee shares their screen and shows where a conflict and a skill live today. The sponsor writes two things before the session ends: the name of the admin who may see every conflict, and whether anyone who staffs work, other than that admin, must see skills. Pro Drive does not demo a Power App.

Bring the notes back. Then run the three tests in `assumptions/assumptions-principia-registers-v1.md`. If the sponsor will not name the employee or the admin, stop. The brief is not ready to build.
